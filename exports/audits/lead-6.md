# Opportunity Memo for Ironbound Roofing & Siding

## Snapshot
Ironbound Roofing & Siding’s website is missing key elements that homeowners rely on to quickly trust and engage with a local roofing service. Without clear calls to action and basic page information, potential customers leave without contacting, costing valuable leads.

## What's Broken
- No page title, so the site doesn’t clearly show up or stand out in search results.
- Missing meta description, which reduces click-throughs from Google and other search engines.
- No visible call to action, leaving visitors unsure how to request a quote or get in touch.
- Page load status is unclear, risking slow or broken user experience that drives visitors away.

## Why It Costs Leads
- Potential customers don’t find the site easily or understand what Ironbound offers, reducing traffic.
- Visitors who do arrive have no clear next step, so they leave without calling or filling out a form.
- The site looks incomplete or unprofessional, which lowers homeowner trust and confidence.
- Slow or broken pages frustrate users, increasing bounce rates and lost opportunities.

## Improvement Angle
We can quickly fix these fundamental issues by adding clear page titles and descriptions to boost search visibility and clicks. Then, we’ll implement strong, easy-to-find calls to action that guide visitors to request a quote or call immediately. This straightforward approach will turn more website visitors into paying customers and grow Ironbound’s local roofing business.

**Recommended Next Move:** Add a sticky click-to-call or quote button on every page.
